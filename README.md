# Dutch Landing Page Prompt (NL Conversion Focus)

Use this prompt when generating CRO recommendations for the Netherlands market:

## Improved Prompt for GitHub/AI

**Role:** Act as a Senior Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Expert and Dutch Market Specialist.

**Task:** Analyze and provide specific, actionable improvements for my landing page \[Insert URL/Code\] to maximize conversions in the Netherlands market.

### Constraints & Context

1. **Current Cultural Context (April/May 2026 NL):** Align the hero section and marketing hooks with:
   - **Sports:** Final stages of the Eredivisie and buildup to the 2026 FIFA World Cup (Dutch team opens in June).
   - **Entertainment:** Buzz around *The Super Mario Galaxy Movie* or the Michael Jackson biopic (April 24).
   - **Holidays:** “Oranje” fever linked to Koningsdag (King’s Day) on April 27.

2. **Localization & Tone:** Reflect the Dutch “Doe maar gewoon” mindset:
   - Direct, transparent, practical language.
   - Avoid overhyped “Americanized” copy.
   - Use informal yet professional Dutch pronouns (`je` vs `u`) based on product context.

3. **Trust Signals:** Recommend Dutch credibility markers where relevant:
   - iDEAL payment support/logo
   - Thuiswinkel Waarborg
   - Trustpilot (or locally relevant verified reviews)

4. **AEO & Speed:** Optimize recommendations for:
   - Answer Engine Optimization (clear Q&A style, concise scannable answers).
   - Mobile-first performance (fast loading, lightweight hero content, clear CTA hierarchy).

### Output Format (Required)

Return a structured list of concrete changes for:

1. **Hero Headline** (must include a current trend reference from the context above)
2. **Social Proof / Trust Section** (localized trust markers and credibility upgrades)
3. **CTA Buttons** (Dutch-market wording and conversion intent)
